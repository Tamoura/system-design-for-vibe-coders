A Practical Guide to Becoming an AI-Native Engineer

Where do you stand?

A self-assessment for A Practical Guide to Becoming an AI-Native Engineer: 60 questions across the course's 15 modules, plus a checklist of what you have actually done. You get a level, a map of strong and weak modules, and the exact lessons to study next.

How it works

  1. Knowledge check — 60 multiple-choice questions, 4 per module, about 20 minutes. Answer without looking things up; unanswered counts as wrong.
  2. Evidence — tick only what you have actually done and verified yourself. "I could do it" is not "I did it".
  3. Results — each module is Not yet, Aware (3 of 4 questions right) or Practised (aware + both evidence items). Your level and the lessons to study next follow from that.

Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you export your result yourself.

Knowledge check

Evidence of practice

Tick only what you have done for real and checked with your own eyes — in the Najm Remit lab repository or your own project, with the result checked by tests or a grader — never with real customer data or secrets.
