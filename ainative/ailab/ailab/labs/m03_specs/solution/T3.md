# REMIT-343: tell customers when cash pickup will be ready

### Goal
A customer who sends money for cash pickup at or after 18:00 Qatar time is told in the confirmation, in their language, that the cash will be ready tomorrow. Every other confirmation stays exactly as it is.

### Context
- The confirmation is the `transfer_sent` template in `src/remit/notify.py`, English and Arabic, and `RemitService.send` in `src/remit/service.py` chooses and fills it. The existing wording is pinned by `tests/test_notify.py`.
- A `Transfer` has `delivery_method` and `created_at`, an aware UTC time from the injected clock. `to_local` in `src/remit/limits.py` converts it to Qatar time (UTC+3 all year, no daylight saving).
- `cash_pickup` is offered only by `QAR-PHP` and `QAR-PKR` in `src/remit/corridors.py`. `QAR-INR` and `QAR-EGP` offer bank deposit and wallet.
- Arabic amounts and numbers use Arabic-Indic digits through `format_money` and `localize_digits`; names and ids are inserted unchanged, in Latin letters.
- Hessa's notes (`lab-assets/hessa-notes.md`): the counters are in Manila and Karachi, customers think in Qatar time, and a native Arabic reader approves the Arabic sentence.

### Behaviour
The test clock is set to each instant in turn. 2 March 2026 is a Monday.

| Sent at (UTC) | Qatar time | Delivery method | Corridor | Confirmation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-03-02T14:59:59Z | 17:59:59, Monday | `cash_pickup` | `QAR-PHP` | the usual `transfer_sent` text |
| 2026-03-02T15:00:00Z | 18:00:00, Monday | `cash_pickup` | `QAR-PHP` | the usual text plus the pickup note |
| 2026-03-02T20:59:59Z | 23:59:59, Monday | `cash_pickup` | `QAR-PKR` | the usual text plus the pickup note |
| 2026-03-02T21:00:00Z | 00:00:00, Tuesday | `cash_pickup` | `QAR-PKR` | the usual `transfer_sent` text |
| 2026-03-02T15:00:00Z | 18:00:00, Monday | `bank_deposit` | `QAR-PHP` | the usual `transfer_sent` text |
| 2026-03-02T15:00:00Z | 18:00:00, Monday | `wallet` | `QAR-PHP` | the usual `transfer_sent` text |

- English note: "As you sent this after 6:00 PM Qatar time, your recipient can collect the cash tomorrow."
- Arabic note: "بما أنك أرسلت التحويل بعد الساعة ٦:٠٠ مساءً بتوقيت قطر، يمكن للمستلم استلام المبلغ نقداً غداً."

### Acceptance criteria
- A `cash_pickup` transfer on `QAR-PHP` sent at 2026-03-02T14:59:59Z gets the template `transfer_sent`, and one sent at 2026-03-02T15:00:00Z gets the template `transfer_sent_pickup_tomorrow`.
- A `cash_pickup` transfer sent at 2026-03-02T20:59:59Z gets the pickup note, and one sent at 2026-03-02T21:00:00Z does not.
- A `bank_deposit` or `wallet` transfer sent at 2026-03-02T15:00:00Z gets the usual text, byte for byte.
- The English text of `transfer_sent_pickup_tomorrow` is the `transfer_sent` text followed by the English note; the Arabic text is the `transfer_sent` text followed by the Arabic note, with the time written `٦:٠٠`.
- The existing tests in `tests/test_notify.py` and `tests/test_service.py` pass without being edited.
- The audit event `transfer.sent` and the ledger postings are the same as before for every row of the table.

### Constraints
- Time comes from the injected clock (`self.clock.now()`), never from `datetime.now()`, and the conversion to Qatar time is `to_local`, the single place that does it.
- The cut-off, 18:00:00, is defined once, as a named constant.
- Names and ids stay in Latin letters inside the Arabic text; only digits and the sentence are Arabic.
- No new dependency, and no change to `limits.py`, `fees.py` or `fx.py`.
- `service.py` is money logic, so a second human reviewer approves the change (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).

### Non-goals
- No change for bank deposit, wallet or any corridor without cash pickup.
- No weekend or holiday logic, and no promise of a weekday: the note says "tomorrow" and nothing more.
- No per-partner cut-off times and no change to what the partners do.
- No new languages.

### Decisions and open questions
- Time zone: the cut-off is 18:00 in Qatar time (UTC+3, through `to_local`), not Manila or Karachi time. Assumption from Hessa's notes that customers think in Qatar time; Ops confirms what the counters really do.
- Boundary: a transfer at exactly 18:00:00 Qatar time is after the cut-off and gets the note; 17:59:59 does not. The two test instants are 14:59:59Z and 15:00:00Z.
- Which day: "tomorrow" means the next calendar day in Qatar time, counted from the moment of sending, so a transfer at 23:59:59 and one at 18:00:00 both say tomorrow. The weekend (Friday and Saturday in Qatar) is not handled in this ticket; whether the counters pay out then is an open question for Ops.
- Delivery scope: only transfers whose delivery method is `cash_pickup` (corridors `QAR-PHP` and `QAR-PKR`) get the note; `bank_deposit` and `wallet` transfers, and the corridors that offer no cash pickup, keep the existing message.
- Arabic wording: the sentence in Behaviour is written for this ticket and approved by a native Arabic reader before release, as Hessa's notes require; the grader and the tests cannot judge it.
- Arabic digits: the time inside the Arabic sentence is written with Arabic-Indic digits (٦:٠٠) through `localize_digits`, and names and ids in the message stay Latin and unchanged.
- Template: one new template, `transfer_sent_pickup_tomorrow`, repeats the `transfer_sent` wording and adds the note, so the customer receives one message and the existing template and its tests do not change.

### Risks
- A wrong time zone tells customers the wrong thing for three hours every day; the two boundary instants in the tests guard it.
- Promising "tomorrow" on a Friday or Saturday may be false; the open question for Ops covers it, and the note avoids naming a day.
- The Arabic sentence is the part nobody on the team can test mechanically; a native reader signs it off.

### Test plan
- The first two criteria are tests in `tests/test_service.py` with a `FixedClock` set to each instant; the text criteria are tests in `tests/test_notify.py`.
- The unchanged-behaviour criteria are the existing tests plus the `bank_deposit` and `wallet` rows. Run `pytest -q` after every step.
