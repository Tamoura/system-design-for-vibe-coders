# REMIT-341: 15% off our fee for transfers to Egypt

### Goal
Customers who send QAR to Egypt (corridor `QAR-EGP`) pay 15% less in fees. They see the saving on the quote and read it in the confirmation message, in English and in Arabic. Nobody is charged a fee that differs from the fee on their quote.

### Context
- The fee for `QAR-EGP` comes from schedule `STD-B` in `src/remit/fees.py`: a tier chosen by the whole amount (upper bound inclusive), then `base = min(max(raw, 6.00), 120.00)`, then the express surcharge of 20.00 is added after the cap. `explain_fee` shows the working and `quote_fee` returns the total.
- `STD-B` also prices `QAR-PKR`, and `tests/test_fees.py` pins the Pakistan fees (15.50 for 500.00, 6.00 for 100.00, 120.00 capped).
- `RemitService.quote` and `RemitService.send` in `src/remit/service.py` both call `quote_fee`; `send(quote=...)` reuses the fee the customer was shown.
- The confirmation is the `transfer_sent` template in `src/remit/notify.py`, English and Arabic. Arabic amounts use Arabic-Indic digits through `format_money`.
- Hessa's notes (`lab-assets/hessa-notes.md`): "15% off what the customer would normally pay us in fees"; support must be able to explain every number on a quote.

### Behaviour
Corridor `QAR-EGP`, schedule `STD-B` (minimum 6.00, cap 120.00, express surcharge 20.00). The discounted base is the base fee after the minimum and the cap, times 0.85, rounded half up to 0.01. The saving is the base fee minus the discounted base.

| Send amount (QAR) | Express | Base fee today | Discounted base | Saving | Fee charged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100.00 | no | 6.00 (minimum applied) | 5.10 | 0.90 | 5.10 |
| 260.00 | no | 9.50 | 8.08 (8.075 rounds up) | 1.42 | 8.08 |
| 260.00 | yes | 9.50 | 8.08 | 1.42 | 28.08 |
| 1,000.00 | no | 28.00 | 23.80 | 4.20 | 23.80 |
| 15,000.00 | no | 120.00 (cap applied) | 102.00 | 18.00 | 102.00 |
| 15,000.00 | yes | 120.00 (cap applied) | 102.00 | 18.00 | 122.00 |

- Given a transfer of 500.00 QAR to `QAR-PKR`, then the fee is 15.50 QAR, as today. Given 500.00 QAR to `QAR-INR`, then the fee is 12.00 QAR, as today.
- Given a customer whose language is `ar` and a transfer of 260.00 QAR to Egypt, then the confirmation contains the saving `١٫٤٢ ريال قطري` and the sentence given under Decisions.

### Acceptance criteria
- `quote_fee(Money("100.00", "QAR"), get_corridor("QAR", "EGP"))` is `Money("5.10", "QAR")`; for 260.00 QAR it is 8.08 QAR; for 15,000.00 QAR it is 102.00 QAR.
- With `express=True` the fee for 260.00 QAR to `QAR-EGP` is 28.08 QAR: the discounted base plus the full 20.00 surcharge.
- `quote_fee` for 500.00 QAR is 15.50 QAR on `QAR-PKR` and 12.00 QAR on `QAR-INR`, and the existing tests in `tests/test_fees.py` pass without being edited.
- For every amount from 100.00 to 15,000.00 QAR in steps of 0.01, discounted base plus saving equals the base fee to the cent, and the discounted base is never above the base fee.
- `RemitService.quote` for 260.00 QAR to EGP returns a fee of 8.08 QAR, and `RemitService.send(..., quote=quote)` stores `Transfer.fee == Money("8.08", "QAR")` and debits 268.08 QAR.
- The English `transfer_sent` text for that transfer ends with "You saved 1.42 QAR with our Egypt offer." and the Arabic text contains `١٫٤٢ ريال قطري`.
- `python -m remit quote QAR 260 EGP` prints a line `Saving: 1.42 QAR`, and `python -m remit quote QAR 260 INR` prints exactly what it printed before.

### Constraints
- Money is `Decimal` through `Money`; no floats. Rounding is half up, as everywhere in Remit.
- Change only `src/remit/fees.py`, `src/remit/service.py`, `src/remit/notify.py`, `src/remit/cli.py` and their tests. Do not touch `money.py`, `fx.py`, `limits.py` or `ledger.py`.
- No new dependency. Time and ids stay with najmkit and the injected clock.
- Money logic needs a second human reviewer (`CONTRIBUTING.md`), and the pull request is labelled `AI-assisted` if an agent helped.
- The discount percentage lives in one constant, so that switching the offer off is a one-line change.

### Non-goals
- No other corridor, no start or end date, and no time window: the offer runs until the constant is set to zero.
- No change to exchange rates, margins, limits or the ledger postings beyond the fee they already receive.
- No customer-specific discounts: `Customer.loyalty_tier` stays unused.
- No redesign of the quote output beyond one extra line.

### Decisions and open questions
- Express surcharge: the 15% applies to the base fee only, and the express surcharge of 20.00 is not discounted. Assumption: "our fee" in the ticket is the fee schedule, and Hessa's notes do not mention express. Hessa confirms.
- Minimum fee: the discount is applied after the minimum and the cap, so the discounted fee can fall below the schedule minimum of 6.00 (100.00 QAR costs 5.10 QAR). Assumption: marketing wants the full 15% to show on small transfers too. Finance has not seen this, so Hessa confirms with Finance.
- Rounding: the discounted base is the base fee times 0.85 as a Decimal, rounded half up to 0.01 (8.075 becomes 8.08), and the saving is the base fee minus the discounted base, never rounded on its own, so the numbers on the quote always add up.
- Scope: the discount is keyed on the corridor `QAR-EGP`, not on the schedule `STD-B`, because `QAR-PKR` shares `STD-B` and must not change. It applies to every KYC tier and every delivery method.
- Wording: the English sentence is "You saved 1.42 QAR with our Egypt offer." The Arabic sentence is "وفّرت ١٫٤٢ ريال قطري بفضل عرضنا على التحويلات إلى مصر." A native Arabic reader approves it before release, as Hessa's notes require. The amount in the Arabic sentence comes from `format_money`, so its digits are Arabic-Indic.
- Quote and charge: the saving and the fee come from the same calculation as the quote, so a transfer sent with `quote=quote` is charged exactly what the quote showed.

### Risks
- A discount keyed on `STD-B` would undercharge every Pakistan transfer; the unchanged Pakistan tests in `tests/test_fees.py` would catch it.
- Revenue: every Egypt transfer earns less. Finance should see the numbers before launch.
- The saving shown on the quote and the fee debited could drift apart if they were calculated twice; one test compares them end to end.
