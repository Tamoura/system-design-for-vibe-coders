# INC-2031: the title from the record

Severity, service, date and incident commander, exactly as the record gives them.

## Summary

Two or three sentences: what happened, for how long, with what effect. Facts only.

## Impact

Who and what was affected, with the numbers the record gives (and no others; it has counts, no percentages). A figure you work out cites the events it comes from.

## Timeline

Use the record's times (UTC) and cite its event ids. One row per event, time first; cover at least nine of the thirteen events, not three highlights.

| Time (UTC) | What happened | Record id |
|---|---|---|
| hh:mm | ... | E? |

## What we know

Facts that the record states, each with the id or field it comes from.

## What we infer

Reasoning that goes beyond the record (at least two inferences). For each: what it rests on, how
confident you are (low, medium, high) and what would confirm or refute it.

## What we do not know

At least two questions the record cannot answer. Leave them open.

## Action items

What the team will do, as a list of at least three items. Each item has an owner (a role or a team) and a way to see that it is done.
