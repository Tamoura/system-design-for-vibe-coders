# N. Short title: a noun phrase that names the decision

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Status

Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated or Superseded by ADR N (pick one, and say why if it is not obvious).

## Context

The forces at play: what is true about the code, the customers and the rules today.
Point at the code with `path` or `module.name` in backticks, so a reader can check you.

## Decision

We will ... (one or two sentences, in the active voice).

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to live with.
Write the downsides too, not only the benefits.

## Critic's objections

Ask a critic (an AI assistant, a colleague, you wearing a different hat) to attack the
decision and to make the best case for the strongest alternative. Record each objection
and what you did about it.
