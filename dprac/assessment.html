Digital Practice

Where do you stand?

A self-assessment for Digital Practice: 56 questions across the course's 14 modules, plus a checklist of what you have actually done. You get a level, a map of strong and weak modules, and the exact lessons to study next.

How it works

  1. Knowledge check — 56 multiple-choice questions, 4 per module, about 20 minutes. Answer without looking things up; unanswered counts as wrong.
  2. Evidence — tick only what you have actually done and verified yourself. "I could do it" is not "I did it".
  3. Results — each module is Not yet, Aware (3 of 4 questions right) or Practised (aware + both evidence items). Your level and the lessons to study next follow from that.

Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you export your result yourself.

Knowledge check

Pick the best answer. You can change answers until you open your results.

Evidence of practice

Tick only what you have done for real and checked with your own eyes — for real, with something a reviewer could open — a sketch, a test plan, a runbook, a board, a cost sheet — not just planned.

Group view — for teachers and team leads

Load the JSON results your learners exported to see who is where and which modules the group is weakest in. Files are read in this browser only. Results are self-reported and not verified.
