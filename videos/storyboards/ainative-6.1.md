## The agent that agreed with you (lesson 6.1)

**Why this lesson:** It shows sycophancy instead of describing it: a leading question, an agent that agrees, a green suite, and a harmful 'fix' (ainative/modules/06-debug.md:22).
**Target viewer:** A junior to mid-level engineer who has started using a coding agent for debugging.
