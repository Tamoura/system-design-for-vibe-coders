## The Phone in the Lake

**Lesson:** 7.3 Key-person risk: access, backups and a 30-minute handover pack
**Why this lesson:** One ordinary accident shows, as a falling chain, how much of a one-person AI business hangs on one person's access.
**Target viewer:** A solo founder who runs a small product mostly alone, with AI agents doing much of the work.
**Length:** about 80 seconds

## Scenes

**1. 0-8 s - Cold open**
- On screen: A sunny holiday scene. A phone slips from a hand and sinks into a lake with a ripple.
- Visual/animation: Slow sink, bubbles, then the phone screen fades to black underwater.
- On-screen text: "Tick has run for six months."
- VO (EN): "Tick has run for six months. Then one day on holiday, Layla's phone falls into a lake."
- VO (AR): "مرّت ستة أشهر على تشغيل Tick. ثم في يوم إجازة، سقط هاتف ليلى في البحيرة."

**2. 8-20 s - The first dominoes**
- On screen: A row of labelled dominoes: "Message provider", "Password manager", "Domain registrar".
- Visual/animation: The phone is the first domino. It tips the message provider (a code sent to the lost phone), then the password manager (its sign-in lives on that phone). The registrar domino lights up with an envelope icon pointing to an old, locked inbox.
- On-screen text: "One phone = the only second factor"
- VO (EN): "That phone was her only second factor. The message provider wants a code sent to it. Her password manager signs in through it. Her domain registrar emails an address she can no longer open."
- VO (AR): "كان ذلك الهاتف وسيلة التحقق الثانية الوحيدة لديها. مزوّد الرسائل يطلب رمزًا يُرسل إليه، ومدير كلمات المرور يُفتح عن طريقه، ومسجّل النطاق يراسل بريدًا قديمًا لم تعد تستطيع فتحه."

**3. 20-32 s - Nine days**
- On screen: A calendar flipping through nine days. Reminder bubbles keep flying out, then a credit card icon turns red with an "expired" stamp and the bubbles stop.
- Visual/animation: The last domino, labelled "Nadia's salon", falls. An empty appointment book.
- On-screen text: "9 days locked out"
- VO (EN): "For nine days she can't get in. Then the card on the message account expires, sending stops, and Nadia's salon misses a day of reminders."
- VO (AR): "تسعة أيام لا تستطيع فيها الدخول. ثم تنتهي صلاحية البطاقة المسجّلة في حساب الرسائل، فيتوقف الإرسال، ويمرّ يوم كامل على صالون نادية بلا تذكيرات."

**4. 32-42 s - Nobody else can help**
- On screen: Mona stands at a locked door with no key. Around Layla, chat bubbles with an AI assistant are sealed inside a glass jar.
- Visual/animation: Mona's hand reaches for the door handle; a lock icon pulses.
- On-screen text: "No access. No instructions."
- VO (EN): "Mona, the one person Layla trusts, has no access and no instructions. And much of what the business knows is sitting in chats with an AI assistant that nobody else can read."
- VO (AR): "منى، الشخص الوحيد الذي تثق به ليلى، لا تملك صلاحية دخول ولا تعليمات. وكثير مما تعرفه الشركة محفوظ في محادثات مع مساعد ذكاء اصطناعي لا يقرؤها أحد غير ليلى."

**5. 42-62 s - Four fixes**
- On screen: Four tiles appear one at a time: Access, Backups, Knowledge, Authority.
- Visual/animation: Each tile snaps a support under the domino row: a second key (Access), a copy box outside the original account with a tick for "restored once" (Backups), a written page (Knowledge), Mona with a badge (Authority).
- On-screen text: "Access · Backups · Knowledge · Authority"
- VO (EN): "The lesson fixes four things: more than one trusted way into every account that matters; a backup kept somewhere else that you've actually restored once; the facts a stranger needs, written down; and a named person who may act, within limits, if you can't."
- VO (AR): "يعالج الدرس أربعة أمور: أكثر من طريق موثوق للدخول إلى كل حساب مهم، ونسخة احتياطية محفوظة في مكان آخر جرّبت استعادتها فعلًا مرة واحدة، والمعلومات التي يحتاجها شخص غريب مكتوبةً، وشخص محدد يحق له التصرف ضمن حدود إن لم تستطع أنت."

**6. 62-72 s - The trap**
- On screen: A handover pack folder. A sticky note reading "passwords" is pulled out and crossed through; it is replaced by a map pin labelled "where secrets are kept".
- Visual/animation: Red cross over the sticky note, green map pin slides in.
- On-screen text: "Never put the passwords in the pack"
- VO (EN): "The biggest trap is writing the passwords into the handover pack. The pack says where the secrets are and how to get them. It never contains them."
- VO (AR): "أكبر فخ أن تكتب كلمات المرور داخل حزمة التسليم. الحزمة تقول أين توجد الأسرار وكيف يُوصل إليها، ولا تحتويها أبدًا."

**7. 72-80 s - Closing card**
- On screen: The domino row stands again, with a second key beside the first.
- Visual/animation: The phone domino wobbles but nothing else falls.
- On-screen text: "No single person, device or account may be the only way in."
- VO (EN): "No single person, device or account may be the only way in. Open lesson 7.3 and build your thirty-minute handover pack."
- VO (AR): "لا يجوز أن يكون شخص واحد أو جهاز واحد أو حساب واحد هو الطريق الوحيد للدخول. افتح الدرس 7.3 وجهّز حزمة التسليم في ثلاثين دقيقة."

## Takeaway and call to action
Takeaway: no single person, device or account may be the only way in. CTA: open lesson 7.3.
