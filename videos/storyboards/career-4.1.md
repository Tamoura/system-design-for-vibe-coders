**Pick 2 of 3 for this course.** The lesson shows exactly what the parser did to Omar's CV (name in the wrong field, skills section empty), and the before/after split screen is very visual. It ends on one action anyone can take: the plain-text paste test.

## What the ATS saw
**Lesson:** 4.1 A CV that survives the ATS and the human scan (career/modules/04-getting-seen.md)
**Why this lesson:** a concrete machine failure instead of myths, honestly hedged, with a single test the viewer can run today.
**Target viewer:** graduates sending CVs and hearing nothing back.
**Length:** about 80 s

**Closing takeaway:** make true evidence easy for machines and tired humans to read; run the paste test before every new layout.
**Call to action:** open lesson 4.1.
**Re-check before production:** do not put '6 seconds' on screen as fact. Ladders reported about 6 s in 2012 and 7.4 s in 2018, and the lesson itself says to treat the number loosely. Retitle first (04-getting-seen.md:9). Keep ATS behaviour generic and name no vendor.
