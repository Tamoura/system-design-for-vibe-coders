The idea is spatial, so it animates well: a horizontal cut across six columns instead of a deep dig down one. ## Six features in five weeks, or one thin slice

**Lesson:** 2.2 The smallest useful product: scope, prototypes and learning
**Why this lesson:** It answers a question every beginner meets early, "what should we build first?", with a picture rather than a slogan, and it corrects the common misreading of "minimum viable product" as "a cheap, buggy version 1".
**Target viewer:** A new developer, designer or product person, or a career changer building a first app, who is about to scope a first release.
**Length:** about 85 seconds

## Takeaway and call to action
Takeaway: a first product should do one job end to end for one group, teach you the next most important thing, and still be safe to use; cut across the whole journey, not down one step. CTA: open lesson 2.2, draw a story map for an everyday service and cut its first slice.
