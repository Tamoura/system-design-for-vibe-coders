## Delete the column, keep the bias (Lesson 5.1: Non-discrimination in hiring, credit and services)

**Why this lesson:** It shows how AI discriminates through proxies, and why 'the model is accurate' or 'we bought it from a vendor' is no defence (05-other-laws.md:17-24).
**Target viewer:** HR, credit, compliance and product staff who buy or approve AI screening tools.
**Length:** about 80 seconds.

**Closing takeaway:** Removing the protected field does not remove the bias. Test outcomes by group and look for proxies.
**Call to action:** Open Lesson 5.1 and compute the selection-rate ratios yourself.
