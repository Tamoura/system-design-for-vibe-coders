# Storyboard: Founder's Playbook for AI-First Builders, lesson 2.3

> AI-drafted, not expert-reviewed. Part of the November 2026 content audit (`academy/audits/2026-11-content-audit.md`). Check every fact against the lesson and its claims register before production.

**Pick 3 of 3 for this course.** It shows the skill a non-coding builder most lacks: an agent's 'Done' is a claim, not evidence. Three concrete pilot-day failures appear over a green tick, and the closing rule is memorable and will not date.

## 'Done' Is a Claim

**Lesson:** 2.3 Directing an agent to a first version, and checking it
**Why this lesson:** One clean 'Done' tick gets broken by three believable real-world cases. The lesson's rule then turns into a habit the viewer can use the same day.
**Target viewer:** A founder who directs an AI coding agent and cannot read the code it writes.
**Length:** about 75 seconds

## Scenes

**1. 0-10 s - The brief**
- On screen: A chat window. Layla types "Build the reminder sender." A progress bar runs, then the agent replies "Done. Reminders are sent the day before each appointment." A big green tick appears.
- Visual/animation: The tick bounces in with a satisfying sound.
- On-screen text: "Agent: Done ✓"
- VO (EN): "Layla asks her agent to build the reminder sender. An hour later: Done."
- VO (AR): "تطلب ليلى من وكيل البرمجة أن يبني خدمة إرسال التذكيرات. وبعد ساعة يردّ: تمّ."

**2. 10-20 s - The happy path**
- On screen: Layla's own phone buzzes with a test reminder, then a few tidy sample records she typed herself.
- Visual/animation: Clean, neatly aligned rows with a soft glow.
- On-screen text: "Tested with her own number"
- VO (EN): "She tries it with her own number and an appointment she created. A message arrives. She tells Nadia it's ready for a small pilot."
- VO (AR): "تجرّبه برقمها وبموعد أنشأته بنفسها، فتصل الرسالة. فتخبر نادية أنه جاهز لتجربة صغيرة."

**3. 20-42 s - Pilot day**
- On screen: Real salon data pours in. Three red warning cards pop up over the green tick, one at a time: "Reminder for a cancelled appointment", "Sent at 2 a.m.: server clock ≠ salon clock", "Two copies: client entered twice".
- Visual/animation: Each card cracks the green tick a little more; on the third, it turns grey.
- On-screen text: "Cancelled · 2 a.m. · Duplicate"
- VO (EN): "On the first pilot day it reminds a client whose appointment was cancelled that morning. It sends one at 2 a.m. because the server's clock and the salon's clock disagreed. And it texts a client twice because she was entered twice."
- VO (AR): "في أول يوم من التجربة، يذكّر عميلةً أُلغي موعدها صباح ذلك اليوم، ويرسل تذكيرًا في الثانية فجرًا لأن ساعة الخادم لا تطابق ساعة الصالون، ويراسل عميلة مرتين لأنها سُجّلت مرتين."

**4. 42-52 s - Nobody lied**
- On screen: Split screen: on the left, "What the code was written to do" with a tick; on the right, "What the customer needs" with a question mark held by Layla.
- Visual/animation: The question mark slides into Layla's hand.
- On-screen text: "The agent wasn't lying."
- VO (EN): "The agent wasn't lying. The code did what it was written to do. Whether that's what the customer needs is Layla's question, and only she can ask it."
- VO (AR): "لم يكن الوكيل يكذب؛ فقد فعلت الشيفرة ما كُتبت لتفعله. أما هل هذا ما تحتاجه العميلة، فذلك سؤال ليلى، ولا يطرحه أحد سواها."

**5. 52-64 s - The check**
- On screen: A check table with three columns: Pass, Fail, Not checked. One row is filled in as "Not checked" with a reason.
- Visual/animation: Rows fill in; the honest 'Not checked' row is highlighted rather than hidden.
- On-screen text: "Small steps · realistic data · say what you didn't check"
- VO (EN): "So work in small steps, test on realistic data, and write down what you saw with your own eyes, including what you didn't check."
- VO (AR): "لذلك اعمل بخطوات صغيرة، واختبر ببيانات واقعية، ودوّن ما رأيته بعينيك، بما في ذلك ما لم تتحقق منه."

**6. 64-75 s - Closing card**
- On screen: The green tick returns with a small evidence list attached beneath it.
- Visual/animation: The tick and its list lock together.
- On-screen text: "'It works' means 'I tried the cases that could break it, and I can show you'."
- VO (EN): "'It works' means 'I tried the cases that could break it, and I can show you.' Open lesson 2.3 to write your first brief and check."
- VO (AR): "«إنه يعمل» تعني «جرّبتُ الحالات التي قد تُفسده، وأستطيع أن أريك». افتح الدرس 2.3 لتكتب أول تكليف لوكيلك وأول فحص لعمله."

## Takeaway and call to action
Takeaway: an agent saying 'done' is a claim, not evidence. CTA: open lesson 2.3.
