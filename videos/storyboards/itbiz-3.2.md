## Same four jobs, three different bills

**Lesson:** 3.2 Prioritising and funding work: business cases, cost of delay and funding products not projects
**Why this lesson:** "Urgent" becomes a number people can challenge, and the order of work visibly changes the cost. The punchline links back to funding: a three-week feature waited a quarter for money.
**Target viewer:** A product manager, portfolio manager or IT finance partner who orders a backlog or argues for funding.
**Length:** about 85 seconds

## Takeaway and call to action
Takeaway: when team capacity is the constraint, the order of work changes the cost of waiting; do the highest cost of delay divided by duration first, and fund the team rather than each change. CTA: open lesson 3.2 and order five items by CD3.

## Re-check before production
- Recomputed from the lesson's CD3 table (`itbiz/modules/03-portfolio.md`, Going deeper), cost of waiting = weekly cost of delay × finishing week. CD3 order: 30 × 3 + 8 × 4 + 50 × 14 + 20 × 22 = 90 + 32 + 700 + 440 = 1,262. Biggest first: 50 × 10 + 30 × 13 + 20 × 21 + 8 × 22 = 500 + 390 + 420 + 176 = 1,486. Shortest first: 8 × 1 + 30 × 4 + 20 × 12 + 50 × 22 = 8 + 120 + 240 + 1,100 = 1,468. The difference of 224 is computed here, not stated in the lesson.
