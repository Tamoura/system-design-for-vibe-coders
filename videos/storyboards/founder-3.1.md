## The Cost You Weren't Watching

**Lesson:** 3.1 Unit economics in plain words, including AI and model costs
**Why this lesson:** The cost sheet builds row by row, and its punchline runs against intuition for AI builders. Every figure is in the lesson and has been recomputed.
**Target viewer:** A founder who has built an AI-powered product and is about to set or defend a price.
**Length:** about 85 seconds

## Takeaway and call to action
Takeaway: count every use as a cost. The AI line is rarely the only one that matters. CTA: open lesson 3.1.
