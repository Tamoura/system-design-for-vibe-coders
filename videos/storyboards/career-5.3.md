## 99% accurate, and useless
**Lesson:** 5.3 System design, data and ML interviews for juniors (career/modules/05-interviews.md)
**Why this lesson:** the accuracy paradox is better shown than told, it will not date, and it ends on a single method.
**Target viewer:** final-year CS / data science students preparing for data-science or ML interviews.
**Length:** about 75 s
