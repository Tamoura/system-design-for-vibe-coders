## 99% accurate, 8% right (lesson 1.2)

**Why this lesson:** the base-rate trap is the course's strongest shown-not-told idea (aipm/modules/01-ai-literacy.md:264).
**Target viewer:** a non-technical PM or sponsor who is about to be shown a '99% accurate' AI model.
**Length:** about 75 s.

| # | Seconds | On screen | Visual metaphor / animation | On-screen text (EN) | Voice-over (EN) | Voice-over (AR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-8 | A vendor slide with a big green '99% accurate' | Slide stamps in, confetti starts, then freezes | 99% accurate! | Your fraud model is ninety-nine percent accurate. Great news? | نموذج كشف الاحتيال لديك دقيق بنسبة تسعة وتسعين في المئة. خبر رائع؟ |
| 2 | 8-18 | A field of grey dots | Zoom out to a dense field; counter ticks to 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 transactions | Picture a million card transactions at Najm Bank. | تخيّل مليون عملية بطاقة في بنك نجم. |

Ask which error hurts more. | في الأحداث النادرة، الصحة الإجمالية ليست المقياس الذي يُعتمد عليه. اسأل: أي خطأ يؤلم أكثر؟ |

**Closing takeaway:** for rare events, look at precision and recall, not accuracy.
**Call to action:** 'Open lesson 1.2, Capabilities and limits, and recompute the alerts when false alarms drop to 0.2%.'
