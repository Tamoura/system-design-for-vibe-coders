**Pick 3 of 3 for this course.** A key that was 'deleted' at HEAD but is still in history is a show-don't-tell reveal. It corrects a common and harmful belief with the right order of steps, and it matters to anyone building with AI agents.

## Deleted is not gone
**Lesson:** 3.2 Your GitHub, READMEs and writing about your work (career/modules/03-portfolio.md)
**Why this lesson:** a scrub-back through the commit timeline makes the danger visible, and the fix is three ordered steps.
**Target viewer:** graduates with public portfolio repos, especially those built with AI coding agents.
**Length:** about 70 s
