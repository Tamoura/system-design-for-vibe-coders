## The cheap model is the expensive one (lesson 8.2)

**Why this lesson:** the 'cost per resolved conversation' flip overturns the most common AI cost intuition (08-metrics.md:298).
**Target viewer:** a PM or finance sponsor choosing between a large and a small model.
**Length:** about 80 s.

**Closing takeaway:** measure cost per outcome, because handoffs can dwarf model cost.
**Call to action:** 'Open lesson 8.2, Unit economics, and rerun the table with your own handoff cost.'
