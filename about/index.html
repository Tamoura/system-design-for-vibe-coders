How these courses are made

A short, plain account of how this library is written, what has not been checked yet, and how to tell us about a mistake.

Status of every course: drafted with AI, not yet expert-reviewed.

What this is

A free library of 13 courses in English and Arabic, with 447 lessons in all. The courses were written between July and October 2026.

How they are made

  • The owner sets the scope and the rules for each course.
  • AI agents (Claude Code) draft the lessons, check facts using web search, write the quizzes, translate the lessons into Arabic, and check that the Arabic has the same structure as the English.
  • Fact checks often rely on search summaries rather than the full source.
  • The pages are built from Markdown files in a public repository on GitHub.

What has not been done yet

  • No lesson has been reviewed by a named subject expert.
  • No Arabic editor has reviewed the Arabic text.
  • No group of learners has piloted a course.
  • No certificate is issued.

Quality levels

Each course has one level. A level is shown only when the signed records for it exist in the repository. No level means accreditation or endorsement by a certification body.

LevelWhat it means
DraftThe material exists and passes the automated checks. Every course is at this level today.
Technically reviewedA named, qualified expert checked correctness and the findings were fixed.
Assessment validatedOutcomes, rubrics and exam questions were examined independently.
Pilot testedReal learners completed the course, and the results and fixes are recorded.
Professionally validatedLearners' practical work was checked by an independent assessor against a published rubric.

What a self-assessment result shows

  • Knowledge shown: your score on the multiple-choice questions.
  • Practical work declared: the evidence you tick as done. Nobody checks it; it is your own record.
  • Competence validated: needs an independent assessor to review your work. The library does not offer this yet.

Known issues being fixed

  • The AI governance course is being re-checked against changes to EU AI law.
  • The practice exams were rewritten in November 2026 so the correct option can no longer be spotted by its length. No assessment expert has reviewed them yet, so a score is practice, not evidence of readiness for any exam.
  • The course on running AI agents in production is being renamed, and its regulatory section has been removed.

How to report a problem

Course pages have a “Report a problem” link at the bottom. It opens a new issue on GitHub, so you need a GitHub account. You can also report a problem here.
