System Design for Vibe Coders

Where do you stand?

A self-assessment for System Design for Vibe Coders: 56 questions across the course's 14 areas, plus a checklist of what you have actually done. You get a level, a map of strong and weak areas, and the exact lessons to study next.

How it works

  1. Knowledge check — 56 multiple-choice questions, 4 per area, about 20 minutes. Answer without looking things up; unanswered counts as wrong.
  2. Evidence — tick only what you have actually done and verified yourself. "I could do it" is not "I did it".
  3. Results — each area is Not yet, Aware (3 of 4 questions right) or Practised (aware + both evidence items). Your level and the lessons to study next follow from that.

Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you export your result yourself.

Evidence of practice
